Beating the opener

One live experiment: WNBA player props, priced from scratch by a talent model that never sees the market's number, bet on FanDuel and scored on closing-line value first, profit second. The Players tab is the model's current read on every player it prices. Four markets were researched, and the two honest "no"s and the cancelled soccer experiment are kept in Evidence and the Archive.

Running mean CLV per bet. Raw CLV (vs the close as quoted) skews negative by construction: an under-heavy sheet fades a close the books shade toward overs. CLV* re-scores the same bets against the shade-adjusted close (AUDIT N1) and is the fair yardstick.

How to read this

CLV, not profit

Closing-line value is p_close × odds_taken − 1: did the price beat where the market ended up? It converges within a season. ROI needs several.

Priced from scratch

The v3 talent model never sees the market's number. It prices the slate itself and bets FanDuel two-way quotes where its claimed EV > 10%, quarter-Kelly on half the claimed edge.

Two yardsticks

Raw CLV compares to the close as quoted; CLV* to the shade-adjusted close. On an under-heavy sheet the raw number is structurally negative — CLV* is the one that has to reach zero and beyond.

Claim vs outcome

The filter line's model predicted ROI is the stake-weighted ev_claimed of the same settled bets the realized ROI is computed from — the model's own claim at the prices taken. It is positive on every slice by construction (nothing is bet under +10%), so only the gap is informative: the pre-registered expectation is that a claim realizes about half.

Losing can still be a pass

Negative P&L with clearly positive CLV means the model works and variance didn't cooperate. Profit with negative CLV is just luck.

Where these numbers come from

A talent state, not an average

Every game updates a per-minute Kalman state; hot streaks shrink back toward an informed career-curve prior, and the offseason widens uncertainty. Parameters were fit strictly pre-2025 and pinned — this is the same model whose prospective test is running.

Minutes are the hard part

Rates ride on a minutes estimate from recent usage. The hourly news desk turns injury reports into overrides — OUT, or a minutes cap — and the projection scales with them. Chips on a row mark an active override.

The market never leaks in

Projections are priced blind. Lines appear here only as the after-the-fact comparison — the same discipline the backtests were scored under.

Disagreement is not a bet

A gap becomes a pick only through the gates: FanDuel still at its opener, fresh panel, fresh player, claimed EV under 25%. Bigger claims are quarantined as suspected defects.

WNBA props — the anchored era retired

The model that opened the WNBA live experiment anchored on the market's opening price and bet the residual. It was retired on 31 Jul — it inverted its opinion out of the price rather than pricing games itself — and the from-scratch v3 talent model took over the same evening. Its handful of bets stay in the live bet log, left of the "v3 model live" rule on the charts, because the bankroll is continuous. The full post-mortem is in PROGRESS.md and AUDIT.md.
