# soccer (subproject of beating-the-opener)

Soccer 1X2 model that beats the opening line in the Pinnacle era (see
README.md). **No live experiment runs here** — it was cancelled 2026-07-28
before its first bet (no edge in the post-Pinnacle regime), and the model's
anchored architecture was retired repo-wide on 2026-07-31 in favour of
first-principles pricing (../PLAN.md). Don't run src/live_pipeline.py or
score picks unless the user explicitly asks.

live/PROTOCOL.md and the live pipeline are kept as the record of what would
have run. RESULTS.md is auto-generated by src/settle_bets.py - don't edit by
hand.
