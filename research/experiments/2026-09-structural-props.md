Forecast a probability of not playing and a conditional minutes distribution from prior appearances, role, rest and variability. Past possessions, substitutions, foul timing and score margins can improve that mechanism.

## Frozen fitting recipe and iteration budget

Use pre-2015 history for rate priors and warm-up. A 2025 game may update a later game's history only after its assumed availability time. No 2026 source or outcome enters development, even if a caller changes a command-line date.

Recipe 1: exponentially weighted role/minutes history (fast 0.18, slow 0.05), explicit zero-minute appearances for participation, conditional minutes PMF on integer minutes 1–60 with role-specific historical variability and shrinkage to prior-season player/league history; Gaussian Kalman rates per opportunity with positive rate floor; prior team/opponent pace acts on possession opportunity. Conditional count distribution is Poisson or modest fixed extra variance learned from **pre-2025** chronological residuals, with no global count dispersion recalibration on quoted 2025 outcomes. No line or price is used for selecting distributions or fitting any parameter.

Recipe 2 is the same mechanism with stronger prior shrinkage (double the effective prior sample size). It may be attempted only within the two-attempt gate budget. Choose settings on 2023 only. No third fit, no 2025-driven predictor additions, and all attempted results are published. If both 2025 calibration attempts fail, stop the programme before spending on news as specified in the original brief.
